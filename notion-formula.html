How to Use Notion Formula 2.0 Snippets


      

        
Notion formulas turn a plain database into a small app. They can show how many days are left before a deadline, draw a progress bar, color a status, or clean up text. What is Formula 2.0?

      
Formula 2.0 is the current Notion formula language. It adds lists, variables with let, and two writing styles that mean the same thing: function style such as dateBetween(a, b, "days") and dot style such as a.dateBetween(b, "days"). This generator uses function style because it is easier to read and copy.

          Common Notion Formula 2.0 functions and what they do
          
            
FunctionWhat it does

Common mistakes and fixes

      
    
        
  • Property not found. Names are case-sensitive. "due date" and "Due Date" are different properties.
    • 
        
  • Progress bar looks empty or full. A percent-formatted Number property stores 50% as 0.5. If you store 50, pick the 0 to 100 scale.
    • 
        
  • Number plus text error. Wrap numbers in format() before joining them with words. The generator already does this.
    • 
        
  • Date formulas give odd results. Make sure the properties are Date properties, not text.
    • 
        
  • Formula is blank for new rows. Rows with empty dates return an empty result by design in the countdown formula.
    • 
      


      

        Tip: Notion occasionally updates its formula functions. If a pasted formula shows an error, check the property types first, then consult Notion's official formula reference for the latest syntax.
      

    


    
    

      

        
Frequently Asked Questions

        

          

            How do I add a formula to a Notion database? +
            
Open your database, click the plus icon to add a property, choose Formula as the type, then paste the generated formula into the editor and click Done. Make sure the property names in the formula match your database exactly.

          

          

            What is Notion Formula 2.0? +
            
Formula 2.0 is Notion's current formula language. It supports lists, the let function for variables, and both function style and dot style syntax.

          

          

            Why does my progress bar show the wrong length? +
            
A Number property formatted as a percent stores 50% as 0.5. If your property holds whole numbers such as 50, choose the 0 to 100 scale in the generator.

          

          

            Does this generator send my data anywhere? +
            
No. The ZeptoSolve generator runs entirely in your browser. It only builds text for you to copy, and it never connects to your Notion workspace.
