About ZeptoSolve

ZeptoSolve is a growing collection of small, single-purpose web tools. The name comes from "zepto," a tiny unit of measure, because we believe the best tools solve one specific problem quickly and then get out of your way.

Our Notion Formula 2.0 Generator helps students, freelancers and teams create powerful database formulas in seconds. Our Bangladesh Income Tax Calculator gives taxpayers a quick estimate before they file. And our Digital Detox guide reviews feature phones with long battery life and modern Type-C charging for anyone ready to step back from constant scrolling.

Every ZeptoSolve tool is free, privacy-friendly and built as a lightweight static page so it stays fast on any connection.
