How Bangladesh Income Tax Works for AY 2026-27

Bangladesh taxes individuals on a progressive slab system. Income up to the tax-free threshold is not taxed. Each band of income above it is taxed at its own rate, so only the part of your income inside a band pays that band's rate. This ZeptoSolve calculator applies the slabs for you and shows every step.

Minimum tax

When your income is above the tax-free threshold, you generally owe at least a minimum tax, even if the slab calculation produces a smaller figure. The standard minimum is ৳5,000, and first-time taxpayers have a lower minimum of ৳1,000. The calculator applies this automatically and tells you when it has been used.

Salary exemption

Salaried employees can generally exclude one-third of their total salary from tax, up to a fixed ceiling. Choose "Salary income" in the calculator and it subtracts this exempt amount before applying the slabs. If your income comes from other sources, or you already know your taxable income, choose "Taxable income" instead.

Investment tax rebate

Money put into approved investments such as savings certificates, DPS and life insurance premiums can earn a rebate on your tax. The rebate is a percentage of eligible investment and is capped by both a share of your taxable income and a fixed amount. Rules were adjusted in the 2026-27 budget, so this calculator lets you pick between the long-standing rule and the reduced rule.

Worked example

A general taxpayer with taxable income of ৳8,00,000 has the first ৳4,00,000 tax-free. The next ৳3,00,000 is taxed at 10% (৳30,000) and the remaining ৳1,00,000 at 15% (৳15,000). Total tax is ৳45,000, or about ৳3,750 a month.

What this calculator does not cover

  • The surcharge on high net wealth.
  • Non-resident or company taxation.
  • Tax-free allowance for parents of dependent persons with disabilities and other special reliefs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the tax-free income limit in Bangladesh for 2026-27? +

For assessment year 2026-27 the general tax-free threshold is ৳4,00,000. Women, senior citizens aged 65+, and some other categories have a higher threshold. Always confirm the figure for your category with the NBR.

What is the minimum income tax in Bangladesh? +

If your income is above the tax-free threshold, a minimum tax of ৳5,000 generally applies even when the slab calculation gives a lower amount. First-time taxpayers have a lower minimum of ৳1,000.

How is income tax calculated on salary in Bangladesh? +

Taxable salary is generally your total annual salary minus the exempt portion, which is one-third of salary up to a fixed ceiling. The taxable amount is then taxed in slabs from 10% up to 30% after the tax-free threshold.
